Information Collection and Use

For a better experience and to provide core, billing, and advertising functionality while using our Service, the app uses first-party backend services and third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify your device or account pseudonymously, verify purchases, or analyze app usage.

Specifically, this app uses:

  • Google Play Billing, Firebase Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Functions (europe-west1), and Cloud Firestore: To process optional in-app purchases (such as exam_ticket_pack_5) and auto-renewing subscriptions (such as ad_free_monthly and premium_monthly) and to securely manage your entitlements. When you use paid features, Firebase creates a pseudonymous anonymous User ID (UID) and our Cloud Functions (hosted in europe-west1) and Cloud Firestore store your anonymous UID, a SHA-256 hashed obfuscatedAccountId, Google Play purchase tokens, and entitlement balances. Payment details (credit card, billing address) are handled exclusively by Google Play and are never accessible to LocoMotorApps. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics: To understand how users engage with our application (e.g., frequency of use, events within the app, usage patterns, performance data, download source). Firebase Analytics may collect device information (model, OS version), app instance identifiers, and general location information (derived from IP address). Data is processed by Google: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To report crashes and errors, helping us improve app stability. When a crash occurs, Crashlytics collects device state, installation UUIDs, and stack traces solely for diagnosing and fixing issues.
  • Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity): To verify app and device integrity and protect our backend services (Cloud Functions and Firestore) against abuse, tampering, and unauthorized requests. Play Integrity evaluates device and app integrity signals through Google Play Services.
  • Firebase Remote Config & Firebase Realtime Database: To deliver app configuration parameters and update the question database over the network. These requests do not involve collecting personal user profile data by LocoMotorApps.
  • Google AdMob & Ad Mediation Partners: To display advertisements within the free tier of the app. AdMob and its mediation partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as Google's Advertising ID), IP address, general location, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent choice. Our active mediation partners include:
    • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
    • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
    • InMobi: https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy/
    • Liftoff (Vungle): https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/
  • ML Kit On-Device Translation: To provide optional offline text translation on your device. Translation happens locally on your device; however, ML Kit connects to Google servers when downloading requested language models.
  • Google Play In-App Review & Google Play In-App Updates: To allow you to rate the app or check for and install app updates directly via Google Play Services.

Quiz progress and study answers are primarily processed and stored locally on your device. Beyond pseudonymous purchase/entitlement records in Firestore and aggregated analytics/crash reports, LocoMotorApps does not collect your name, email address, or direct contact details within the app.

Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & § 25 TDDDG)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR & § 25(1) TDDDG): Used for storing/accessing device identifiers and processing data for personalized advertising (AdMob and mediation partners) and non-essential analytics. We obtain your consent via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) when you first open the app in applicable regions. You can review, change, or withdraw your consent at any time in the app's Settings screen (under Privacy / Ad Consent options).
  • Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Used to verify Google Play purchases, maintain your anonymous UID, and provide purchased entitlements (exam_ticket_pack_5, ad_free_monthly, premium_monthly) via Firebase Auth, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Firestore.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Used to ensure app stability and bug fixing (Crashlytics), prevent fraud and protect our backend (Firebase App Check / Play Integrity), and deliver essential app updates and question content (Remote Config, Realtime Database, Play In-App Updates).

Log Data

While the app itself does not create traditional web server log files, the third-party services we use (Firebase, Google Play Services, AdMob, and mediation partners) automatically collect technical diagnostic and usage data when you use the Service. This can include your device's IP address, device model, operating system version, app configuration, timestamps, error stack traces, and interaction events.

Content Source

Our app includes practice questions for the Einbürgerungstest based on publicly available materials related to the official test framework for different federal states of Germany.

Data Retention and Data Deletion Policy

Data collected by Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, and mediation partners is retained according to each provider's data retention policies. Purchase tokens, hashed account identifiers (obfuscatedAccountId), and entitlement records in Cloud Firestore are retained as long as necessary to provide your active entitlements, prevent duplicate/fraudulent redemptions, and comply with statutory commercial/tax retention obligations.

Local study data stored on your device may be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. To request deletion of your pseudonymous Firestore/Firebase Auth record, please contact us at locomotorapps@gmail.com.
